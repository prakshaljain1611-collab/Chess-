# ♟️ En Passant – Chess Club Website

A simple and visually engaging **Chess Club Website** created using **HTML and CSS**.
The website showcases the club's journey, events, community, and achievements while providing a clean chess-themed interface.

 **Live Demo:** chess-nine-flame.vercel.app
## 🌟 Features

* 🏠 **Home Section** – Introduction to the En Passant Chess Club.
* ♟️ **About Our Club** – Information about the club and its vision.
* 📈 **Our Journey** – Highlights the club's growth from its formation to participation in tournaments.
* 🏆 **Events Section** – Displays chess events organized by the club.
* 🥇 **Achievements Section** – Shows statistics such as active members, tournaments, championships, and games played.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **HTML5** – Used to structure the webpage.
* **CSS3** – Used for styling, layouts, colors, hover effects, and grid-based sections.

## 🏰 Website Sections

### Home

The homepage introduces the club with the tagline:

> **WHERE STRATEGY MEETS PASSION**

It also provides buttons for exploring the club's journey and upcoming events.

### About Our Club

This section describes the purpose of En Passant Chess Club and highlights three major aspects:

* Community
* Growth
* Excellence

### Our Journey

The journey section presents important milestones:

* **2022** – Club Formation
* **2023** – First Tournament
* **2024** – Growing Strong
* **2024** – New Heights

### Our Events

The website currently showcases events such as:

* **SHEHMAAT** – May 2024
* **ABES Chess Championship** – May 2025
* **KNIGHTMARES** – November 2025

### Our Achievements

The website highlights:

* **150+** Active Members
* **10+** Tournaments Organised
* **10+** Championships Won
* **100+** Games Played

## 🎨 Design

The website uses CSS Grid for arranging major sections and cards. The navigation, main section, journey, events, and achievements sections use grid-based layouts.

The design mainly uses:

* Black background
* White text
* Golden-yellow highlights
* Underline hover effects
* Simple card-based layouts
